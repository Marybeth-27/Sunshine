About EcoLife

A community-driven project that inspires people to protect the environment through small, daily actions.

🌳 🌱 🌍 🌳

Our Purpose

EcoLife exists to raise awareness about environmental problems and provide practical ways to help solve them.

Our Mission

To educate and empower communities to reduce waste, conserve resources and restore nature.

Our Vision

A world where people live in harmony with nature and clean air, water and green spaces are available to everyone.

Eco Tips

Simple habits that make a big difference.

💧

Save Water

Turn off the tap while brushing and avoid wasting water.

💡

Save Electricity

Turn off unused lights and appliances to save energy.

🌳

Plant Trees

Plant native trees and help protect forests.

🛍️

Reduce Plastic

Use reusable bags, bottles and containers.

🧹

Keep Surroundings Clean

Dispose of waste properly and join community cleanups.

Recycling Guide

Click a category to discover the correct waste classification.

🍃

Biodegradable

Waste that decomposes naturally.

  • Fruit and vegetable peels
  • Leaves and garden waste
  • Eggshells
  • Food scraps
♻️

Recyclable

Materials that can be processed into new products.

  • Glass bottles
  • Metal cans
  • Paper and cardboard
  • Clean plastic bottles
🗑️

Residual

Waste that cannot be composted or recycled.

  • Used tissues
  • Candy wrappers
  • Dirty sponges
  • Diapers
⚠️

Special Waste

Items requiring careful handling.

  • Batteries
  • Electronic waste
  • Expired medicines
  • Paint and chemicals
